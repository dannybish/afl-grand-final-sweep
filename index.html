Welcome to the Grand Final Sweep

This app runs a sweep for AFL Grand Final day among family, friends or colleagues — now with a shareable link so entrants can join and self-manage from their own phone. Here's how it works, step by step:

  1. Teams — confirm the two Grand Final teams and their full player lists.
  2. Entrants — set the entry fee. Entrants can join themselves via the Share tab, or you can add them manually here.
  3. Draws — randomly deal every player out across all entrants for the Norm Smith Medal and First Goalscorer categories.
  4. Tips — each entrant tips a team and margin for both half-time and full-time (they can do this themselves once they've joined).
  5. Payments — set your PayID details so entrants can pay and confirm themselves; track who's paid.
  6. Review — check everyone's picks and see the prize pool split before the game.
  7. Results — once the game's done, enter the actual result to reveal the winners.
  8. Share — grab the join link or QR code to send entrants, and set a tip lock time.

Work through the tabs at the top in order — a Next button will appear on each page once you've done enough there to move on. You can always jump back to an earlier tab to make changes.
